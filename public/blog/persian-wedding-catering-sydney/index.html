Wedding catering guide

Planning Persian Catering for Your Wedding in Sydney

By Kookoo Foods, Strathfield South

If you're planning a wedding and want the food to feel like a home-cooked celebration rather than a standard buffet, Persian catering brings something genuinely different to the table — saffron, herbs, slow-cooked stews, and dishes built for sharing. Here's how we usually help couples plan it, and what's worth thinking about before you book.

How many guests should you plan for?

Persian catering in Sydney works well for weddings of almost any size, but the kitchen matters more than the headcount. We cook everything fresh to order at FoodLab in Strathfield South, so we cater from 10 guests up to around 200 at a hall. If your number falls outside that, it's worth asking early — we'd rather tell you honestly than overpromise.

Building a menu your guests will remember

A good Persian wedding spread usually mixes a few different textures and temperatures rather than one big dish. Some of the combinations that work best for us:

  • Zereshk Polo ba Morgh — saffron chicken with barberry rice, a classic centrepiece
  • Kabab Koobideh — grilled minced lamb and beef skewers, always a crowd favourite
  • Ghormeh Sabzi — slow-cooked herb and lamb stew, for guests who want something rich
  • Kashke Bademjan — smoky eggplant dip, a warm starter that's easy to share
  • Tadig — the crispy rice everyone fights over, worth including on its own

You don't need to choose blind — our full menu is online, and you can build your own spread dish by dish rather than picking a fixed package.

Dietary needs, handled properly

Persian cooking naturally includes a lot of vegetarian and vegan options — rice dishes, herb stews, and vegetable-based kookoo (Persian frittata) all fit comfortably alongside the meat dishes. Gluten-free guests are easy to accommodate too. Just tell us the numbers and any allergies when you enquire, and we'll build the spread around them rather than treating it as an afterthought.

Delivery, or food cooked on-site?

Most Sydney weddings we cater are delivered fresh on the day, timed around your ceremony and reception schedule. We deliver across New South Wales, so this works whether your venue is in Strathfield, the Inner West, or further out. Let us know your venue and timing when you enquire, and we'll work the delivery around it.

When to book

For a wedding, we'd suggest reaching out as soon as you have a rough guest number and date — even if the venue isn't locked in yet. It costs nothing to ask: we usually reply with a suggested spread and a price the same day, and there's no pressure to commit on the spot.
